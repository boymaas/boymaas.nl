BioDen Haag

Boy Maas, m4nic on the boards. Entrepreneur, programmer, designer, philosopher and AI enthusiast. Vim, chess, martial arts and music fill the hours between.

Languages, in order of affection: haskell, scheme, ruby, python, clojure, perl, lisp, C/C++, assembler.

Loves: complex dynamic systems, asynchronous agents, science, magic, mystery, colour, sculpture, rain, food, exercise, human behaviour, crypto, hermetism, meditation, hypnosis.

I run a small development studio in Den Haag, founded in March 1998. Clients include Voetbal International, Coca-Cola, Sponsorloterij, De Telegraaf and Telfort BV.
