about

Entrepreneur, programmer, designer, philosopher, AI enthusiast. I write haskell, scheme, ruby, python, clojure, perl, lisp, C/C++ and assembler, in vim. Away from the keyboard: chess, martial arts, music.

I run a small development studio in Den Haag, founded in March 1998. Clients have included Voetbal International, Coca-Cola, Sponsorloterij, De Telegraaf and Telfort BV.
