About

Boy Maas, known online as m4nic. Coder from the terminal generation: vim, chess, martial arts, music. Fond of complex dynamic systems, asynchronous agents, science, colour, rain and the odd bit of hermetism.

Languages haskell, scheme, ruby, python, clojure, perl, lisp, C/C++, assembler.

Studio a small development studio in Den Haag, founded March 1998. Clients include Voetbal International, Coca-Cola, Sponsorloterij, De Telegraaf and Telfort BV.
