┌─bio─ Den Haag ┐

Boy Maas, bitgnosys on the boards. In the terminal for over twenty years, shell tools and neovim. A creator at heart: the pleasure is in bringing a concept to life, and the best projects are the novel ones with uncertain outcomes. Chess lover, martial artist, music between the hours.

Twenty-five years of it. Systems management at Telfort in the nineties; my own shops Innovita, Tagged.nl and Educoncepts; Rails architecture for Digidentity and Nationale Beeldbank; interim CTO at Mobytron; founder of EOS Amsterdam; DeFi and arbitrage in Rust and Solidity; Solana fuzzing for Hubble Protocol; consensus R&D at Terraform Labs; and now JamZig⚡.

Languages today: Rust and Zig, with Odin on the side. Before, in order of affection: haskell, scheme, ruby, python, clojure, perl, lisp, C/C++, assembler. Cryptography I have shipped: Bandersnatch ring VRFs, BLS12-381, Ed25519, Blake2b, Keccak-256, Reed-Solomon erasure coding.

Loves: complex dynamic systems, asynchronous agents, science, magic, mystery, colour, sculpture, rain, food, exercise, human behaviour, crypto, hermetism, meditation, hypnosis.
