┌─bio─ Den Haag ┐

Boy Maas, m4nic on the boards. Entrepreneur, programmer, designer, philosopher. Vim, chess, martial arts and music fill the hours between.

Languages, in order of affection: haskell, scheme, ruby, python, clojure, perl, lisp, C/C++, assembler.
