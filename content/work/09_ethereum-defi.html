The years when DeFi was young and the mempool was a battlefield.

  • An arbitrage bot in Rust with Solidity and Yul contracts, built to compete against Uberbot on the same opportunities.
  • A custom Ethereum P2P client, so the bot heard about mempool events before the RPC did.
  • An ETH token with liquidity mining contracts, as research into incentive design.
  • At Monetashi: Rust data aggregation and high-frequency trading algorithms for the Deribit options market; the Deribit client is open at boymaas/deribit-rs.
