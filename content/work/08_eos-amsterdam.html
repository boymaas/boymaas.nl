--- title: EOS Amsterdam client: founder order: 8 years: 2018 to 2019 summary: Founding block producer candidate for the community launch of EOS, from the Netherlands, in the founding top ten; and a startup designing trading algorithms and bots for crypto exchanges. ---

EOS Amsterdam was one of the founding block producer candidates for the community launch of EOS in 2018, committed to security, block quality and governance, and among the founding top ten. Running a producer meant infrastructure, operations and governance in a network that was inventing its rules as it went.

Alongside it, the company designed trading algorithms and bots for crypto exchanges, the start of the Rust and market work that followed.
