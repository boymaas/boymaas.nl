Tagged.nl recently launched the new game platform "GameBattle" (gamebattle.nl). GameBattle gives consumers the possibility to sharpen their minds and test their skills by playing game battles.

Consumers can play for free and can play for money. Subscribers always play against each other; all games at GameBattle are skill games and belong to the most popular games on the internet (like diamond runner, mah jong, solitaire, word puzzles, pacman etc). Chance and luck are of no influence; skill is the only thing that matters. GameBattle grows every week. At this moment you can play more than 15 different games and arcade classics.

As a newcomer GameBattle aims to open new markets and steal some share from "old school platforms" like worldwinner.com, skilljam.com and king.com. The European operation is started in The Netherlands. Within the coming months there will be versions available in English, German, French and Spanish.

In the Netherlands Gamebattle supports the World Wildlife Fund. A vast portion of all Gamebattle income is donated.

Gamebattle and "Algemeen Dagblad" News Media (Aug 2006)

Tagged started a co-branded platform with AD News Media. AD News Media is promoting Gamebattle online (the co-branded version) and offline in their newspaper (advertisement 1, pdf; advertisement 2, pdf).

Gamebattle and Spele.nl

Tagged started a co-branded platform with Spele.nl. Spele.nl is the largest online gaming link farm, doing 400,000 unique visitors a day (the co-branded version). For more information look at the article in Metro (jpg).

Business model, subscribers and turnover

The business model is simple: a vast portion of the subscribers buys "credits". With these credits they can play for money against other subscribers. When a player starts a battle 20% of the money at stake is charged as battle fee. Free players can generate advertising income by showing them an ad before a game starts.

Used marketing methods

The GameBattle platform is marketed in two ways:

  1. Affiliate partners: webmasters, mail list owners and direct marketing companies are offered a vast portion of the revenues they generate when they promote the platform.
  2. Co-branded GameBattle sites: high traffic websites (news sites, communities, game portals) are offered a co-branded or white label version of GameBattle.
