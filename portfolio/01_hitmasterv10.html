Hitmaster v1.0

Coca-Cola®, Radio538 and Hitkrant

h prevHitmaster v2.0 l nextFreelance projects q backwork

First version of online concept. In this game you are a popmanager. You will have to buy popstars with a budget of one million dollars. To make profit and earn points you will have to buy those stars that will obtain most publicity on radio538 and the Hitkrant. If you get to the top 5, you can start collecting prizes!

An independent review (in Dutch) was published at the time.
