Concept design, project management and prototype development for one of the most advanced market simulator games in europe. This market simulator targeted at the dutch market allows the participant to get used to many of the facets of electronic trading. Stocks, options and derivates are all represented in this game.

To finish this production a strategic alliance was formed with Interpotential (Denmark), a development shop capable of delivering high quality work in a short timeframe.
