Detail

Users can be a soccer manager for a complete football season. They compete to attain a top 10 ranking by playing wisely in the following ways:

  • buying and selling players (trading),
  • answering questions in the field of soccer,
  • challenging other online users for a 1-on-1 challenge.

Features

Transferspel features the following:

  • 100% Flash interface (see screenshots), which enables unique design and interaction methods unseen in traditional HTML interfaces
  • Online messaging
  • Online trading
  • A unique client synchronisation mechanism: if anything in a user's state on the server changes, it is automatically pushed to the client.

Results

Transferspel delivered:

  • 3,000 contenders
  • 15,432 one-on-one quizzes a month (contenders playing each other)
  • 15,211 trades a month (contenders transferring players)
